v4_05 revision 2: seven counter + stopwatch inputs
2026-11-20

Project remains v4_05.xpr. Hardware IDN and client expected version are
"Seq v4_05" (9 bytes), within the UART command reply limit of 15 bytes.
The seven-input reset/address mapping below remains in effect. The IDN
string is a version label, not an independent capability check.

Pin    Counter address  Stopwatch address  Shared reset  SW start  Counter enable  SW done
jb_0   0                6                  6             11        15              15
jb_2   1                7                  5             10        14              14
jb_4   2                11                 13            15        13              10
jb_6   3                12                 12            14        12              9
ja_2   4                10                 2             7         11              11
ja_4   5                8                  4             9         10              13
ja_6   13               9                  3             8         9               12

Reset/start numbers are trigger_out bit positions. Counter enable numbers are
output_ports[1] bit positions. SW done numbers are trigger_level_in positions.
Each shared reset resets BOTH functions on that pin ONLY. All seven pins have
independent reset/start/enable controls. trigger_out[1:0] remain unused.

Counter read addresses 0..4 and stopwatch read addresses 6..10 are unchanged.
Address 5 is ja_4 counter; address 13 is ja_6 counter. Addresses 11/12 are jb_4/6
stopwatches. Address 14 (trigger flags) and 15 (wait residual) are unchanged.
Only the sequencer's address-13 patterns[1:16] readback is removed.
The patterns register, LED drive, manual outputs, UPDATE BITS and READ BITS stay.
No extra physical pin, XDC reassignment or IOSTANDARD change is needed.

The seven existing Clock Wizard outputs are connected (new channels use 6/7).
Clock IP settings remain 800 MHz. Counter and stopwatch module internals, 64-bit
instructions, FIFO format and the 16-bit trigger bus are unchanged.
Only the new top-level connections and Python hardware definitions are required.

Use the updated HardwareDefinition_v4_05.py and ArtyS7_v1_03.py with this firmware.
SequencerProgram_v1_09 and SequencerUtility_v1_04 encoding is unchanged.
Old hand-written programs with numeric reset masks must be reviewed:
  jb_0 counter reset 15 -> 6; jb_2 counter reset 14 -> 5; ja_2 reset 1 -> 2.
  Old bits 15/14 now START jb_4/jb_6 stopwatches.
Read required results before a shared reset. Do not reset a counter while its
same-pin stopwatch is running. The user selected mutually exclusive use.
